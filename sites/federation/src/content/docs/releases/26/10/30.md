## Release / v26.10.30

*Builds: 5864d41 – 8433c06*

- Fixed an issue where dev log entry titles were incorrectly displaying 2026 as the year; bare URLs within entries are now rendered as proper clickable links.
- Governance lexicons are now automatically validated against the AT Protocol Lexicon specification, ensuring published schemas remain conformant and trustworthy.
- Lexicons have been realigned with the GFN-1 schema and RFC-0009 has been finalized, bringing the federation's protocol definitions into a stable, versioned state.
- The feed generator now connects to the AT Protocol firehose via Jetstream, enabling real-time event ingestion across the federation network.
